How to Play Inductive Challenge

1. Study the Left Pair: The two grids on the left demonstrate a logical transformation rule from top grid to bottom grid.

2. Identify the Rule: Rules can include shape substitutions (e.g., all circles become crosses), rotations, color inversions, row/column shifts, or spatial reflections.

3. Select the Correct Candidates: On the right are 4 candidate grids (A, B, C, D). Exactly TWO of these options form a pair that follows the EXACT same transformation rule.

4. Submit & Score: Click two candidate cards to select them and click Submit Selection before time runs out!
